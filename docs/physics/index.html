Physics Background

Core theory behind the rigid-body solver, collision pipeline, and contact constraints.

Rigid Body Dynamics | Collision Detection | Contact Resolution

Rigid Body Dynamics

Per-body state tracks \(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{v},m^{-1},q,\boldsymbol{\omega},\mathbf{I}_{\text{body}}^{-1}\) and force/torque accumulators.

\[ \mathbf{a} = \mathbf{F}\,m^{-1},\qquad \mathbf{v}_{t+\Delta t}=\mathbf{v}_t+\mathbf{a}\,\Delta t,\qquad \mathbf{x}_{t+\Delta t}=\mathbf{x}_t+\mathbf{v}_{t+\Delta t}\,\Delta t \]
\[ \boldsymbol{\alpha}=\mathbf{I}_{\text{world}}^{-1}\boldsymbol{\tau},\qquad \boldsymbol{\omega}_{t+\Delta t}=\boldsymbol{\omega}_t+\boldsymbol{\alpha}\,\Delta t \]
\[ q_{t+\Delta t} = \operatorname{normalize}\left(q_t + \frac{\Delta t}{2}\,\Omega(\boldsymbol{\omega})\,q_t\right) \]
\[ \mathbf{I}_{\text{world}}^{-1} = \mathbf{R}(q)\,\mathbf{I}_{\text{body}}^{-1}\,\mathbf{R}(q)^T \]

Collision Detection

Broadphase generates candidate pairs with world AABBs, then narrowphase runs OBB-vs-OBB SAT for valid candidates.

\[ h = |R|e,\qquad a_{\text{min}}=c-h,\qquad a_{\text{max}}=c+h \]

Sweep-and-prune sorts by \(a_{\text{min},x}\), maintains an active set, and rejects pairs that fail \(y/z\) overlap before SAT.

SAT tests 15 axes: 3 face normals from body A, 3 from body B, and 9 cross-product axes. Any axis with non-positive overlap separates the pair.

\[ \text{overlap} = \min(a_{\text{max}}, b_{\text{max}}) - \max(a_{\text{min}}, b_{\text{min}}) \]

Contact Resolution

Contact constraints are solved with sequential impulses, friction, warm starting, and positional correction passes.

\[ \Delta\lambda_n = -\frac{(1+\epsilon)\,v_{\text{rel}} + b}{k} \]
\[ |\lambda_t| \le \mu\,\lambda_n \]
\[ \mathbf{c} = \frac{\beta\,\max(0,p-p_{\text{tol}})}{m_a^{-1}+m_b^{-1}}\,\mathbf{n} \]

Persistent contacts reuse cached \((\lambda_n,\lambda_t,\hat{\mathbf{t}})\) to improve convergence across frames.
