Math Background

Linear algebra and quaternion algebra used by simulation and rendering.

Linear Algebra and Transforms

A point in \(\mathbb{R}^3\) is embedded in homogeneous coordinates as:

\[ \mathbf{p} = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{pmatrix} \hookrightarrow \tilde{\mathbf{p}} = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \]

Scale, translation, and Euler-angle rotation are composed as:

\[ \mathbf{R} = \mathbf{R}_z(\gamma)\,\mathbf{R}_y(\beta)\,\mathbf{R}_x(\alpha),\qquad \mathbf{M} = \mathbf{T}\,\mathbf{R}\,\mathbf{S} \]

Rigid-body case (\(\mathbf{S}=\mathbf{I}\)):

\[ \mathbf{p}_{\text{world}} = \mathbf{R}\,\mathbf{p}_{\text{local}} + \mathbf{t},\qquad \mathbf{R}^{T}=\mathbf{R}^{-1},\ \det(\mathbf{R})=+1 \]

Velocity of a point on a rigid body:

\[ \mathbf{v}_a = \mathbf{v}_{\text{cm}} + \boldsymbol{\omega}\times\mathbf{r}_a,\qquad \mathbf{r}_a=\mathbf{a}_{\text{world}}-\mathbf{t} \]

Quaternions and Rotation Algebra

Quaternions extend complex numbers for 3D orientation: \[ q = w + x\mathbf{i} + y\mathbf{j} + z\mathbf{k} \]

Division algebra chain: \[ \mathbb{R} \subset \mathbb{C} \subset \mathbb{H} \subset \mathbb{O} \]

  • \(\mathbb{C}\): loses ordered-field compatibility with multiplication.
  • \(\mathbb{H}\): multiplication is non-commutative.
  • \(\mathbb{O}\): non-associative (alternative algebra).

Quaternion basis rules:

\[ \mathbf{i}^2 = \mathbf{j}^2 = \mathbf{k}^2 = \mathbf{i}\mathbf{j}\mathbf{k} = -1 \]

Unit quaternion rotation about axis \(\hat{\mathbf{n}}\) by angle \(\theta\):

\[ q=\left(\cos\!\left(\theta/2\right),\ \hat{\mathbf{n}}\sin\!\left(\theta/2\right)\right),\qquad \mathbf{v}'=q\,\mathbf{v}\,q^{-1} \]

Quaternion composition is order-dependent: \[ q_{\text{total}} = q_2 q_1 \neq q_1 q_2 \]
